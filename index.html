pinterest-analytics-app

A personal tool that reads the analytics of my own Pinterest Business account and shows them in one place.

What it does

pinterest-analytics-app connects to the Pinterest API to work with my own account. Its first feature retrieves performance numbers, so I can review them without opening each pin one by one.

Impressions
How often my pins were shown
Saves
How often my pins were saved
Outbound clicks
Clicks from my pins to my website

Who it is for

It is a single-user tool built and used by the account owner to understand how the content in the account performs. It is not distributed to other users.

About the account

The Pinterest account promotes my original designs, available in my shop at Redbubble.
